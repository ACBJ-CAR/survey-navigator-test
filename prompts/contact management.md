Prompt 13: Contact management

Implement respondent-friendly contact management.

Support:
- Confirm current CEO/top executive.
- Edit an existing executive.
- Add another executive/contact.
- Remove a contact no longer associated with the organization.
- Select or add the best survey contact.

Requirements:
- Do not expose internal record-management instructions.
- Show clear person cards with name, title and available contact details.
- Require confirmation before destructive removal.
- Keep CEO/top executive and survey-contact roles distinct.
- Preserve changes through save/resume.
- Make confirmed contacts available to optional editorial attribution.

Acceptance checklist:
- Add, edit, confirm, remove and select-contact flows work.
- Roles remain distinct.
- Save/resume preserves changes.
- Editorial attribution can use confirmed contacts.
- Tests pass.
